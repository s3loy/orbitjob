Open http://<load-balancer-address>:{{ .Values.adminapi.service.port }}/console/ after the load balancer is provisioned. An empty status means no address is available from Kubernetes yet.
{{- else if eq .Values.adminapi.service.type "NodePort" }}

Open http://<reachable-node-address>:<node-port>/console/ using a node address reachable from your network. Obtain that address from your cluster administrator; Kubernetes does not assign a public node address through this chart.
